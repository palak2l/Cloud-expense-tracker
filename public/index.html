Cloud Expense Tracker

Made by Palak Lodha

College Cloud Computing Mini-Project

Track money the way a cloud app should: private, session-aware, and yours.

Register an account, keep income and expenses in SQLite, watch category charts update from live records, and optionally store receipts in a private Amazon S3 bucket.

  • Secure bcrypt passwords and HTTP-only sessions
  • Budgets, CSV export, and receipt uploads
  • Ready for Ubuntu on Amazon EC2 with Nginx and PM2
